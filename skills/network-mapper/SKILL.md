# Network Mapper

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for contact tiers (Inner Circle/Strategic/Important/Warm), contact profile template, relationship maintenance cadence (weekly/monthly/quarterly), and touchpoint ideas.

**Trigger keywords:** networking, relationship management, contacts, professional network, introduction, CRM, relationship building.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Tracking contacts by title instead of relationship strength
**Symptom**: Tracking contacts by title instead of relationship strength
**Problem**: A CFO you've never spoken to is worth less than a mid-level manager who champions your work internally.
**Solution**: Map relationships by influence and relationship warmth, not just by title or company prestige.
