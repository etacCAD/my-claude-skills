# Crisis Manager

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full RAPID crisis response framework (Recognize → Assess → Plan → Implement → Debrief), severity levels, communication templates for internal/customer/PR, and post-mortem template.

**Trigger keywords:** crisis, PR response, post-mortem, incident, data breach, escalation, legal threat, brand damage, outage, public statement.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Waiting for full information before communicating
**Symptom**: Waiting for full information before communicating
**Problem**: Silence during a crisis is interpreted as guilt or incompetence. Stakeholders fill the void with speculation.
**Solution**: Communicate what you know, what you don't know, and what you're doing to find out — within the first 2 hours.

### 2. Writing apologies without accountability
**Symptom**: Writing apologies without accountability
**Problem**: 'We're sorry you feel that way' or 'We're sorry if anyone was offended' are not apologies — they shift blame to the audience.
**Solution**: Own the specific action that caused harm. 'We made a mistake by X' builds more credibility than passive language.
