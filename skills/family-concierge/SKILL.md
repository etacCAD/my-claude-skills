# Family Concierge

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full important dates tracking list, reminder schedule (4-week/2-week/1-week/day-of), gift ideas framework (spouse/kids/extended family), family member profile template, and weekly family check-in format.

**Trigger keywords:** family, birthday, anniversary, gift ideas, kids, spouse, family event, school event, holiday planning.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Scheduling all errands on the same day
**Symptom**: Scheduling all errands on the same day
**Problem**: Clustering all logistics into one day creates a single point of failure — one delay cascades into a ruined day.
**Solution**: Distribute recurring tasks (grocery, pharmacy, dry cleaning) across different days with slack time built in.
