# Trend Spotter

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full domains to monitor (macro/economic, industry-specific, competitive), intelligence brief template (Must Know/Worth Watching/Emerging Signals/Opportunity), and Sunday night prep brief format.

**Trigger keywords:** trends, macro trends, market intelligence, industry news, tech trends, competitive moves, strategic intelligence, weekly brief.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Confusing noise for trends
**Symptom**: Confusing noise for trends
**Problem**: A single viral tweet or one-week news spike is noise. A trend requires sustained directional movement over weeks or months.
**Solution**: Validate signals across at least 3 independent sources and track for minimum 2-4 weeks before classifying as a trend.
