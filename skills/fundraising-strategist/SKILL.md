# Fundraising Strategist

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for full guidance including fundraising readiness checklist, pitch deck structure (10-12 slides), investor outreach templates (warm intro + cold email), cap table modeling guidelines, investor pipeline tracking template, and tool integrations.

**Trigger keywords:** fundraising, cap table, VC outreach, pitch deck, investor, Series A, Seed round, term sheet, dilution, runway, raise capital.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Optimizing pitch deck design over narrative clarity
**Symptom**: Optimizing pitch deck design over narrative clarity
**Problem**: A beautiful deck with a muddled story still fails. Investors fund the thesis, not the template.
**Solution**: Finalize the verbal narrative (the 3-minute story) before building slides. Each slide should advance one chapter of that story.

### 2. Pitching to tier-1 VCs before validating the story with tier-2
**Symptom**: Pitching to tier-1 VCs before validating the story with tier-2
**Problem**: Burning warm intros to top firms with an unpolished pitch damages your reputation and forecloses future conversations.
**Solution**: Run 5-10 practice pitches with friendly or lower-priority investors first. Incorporate feedback before approaching primary targets.
