# Board Deck Builder

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full board deck structure (15-20 slides), slide design principles, board update email template, and integration with pnl-analyzer/okr-coordinator/cash-flow-modeler.

**Trigger keywords:** board deck, board update, investor presentation, board meeting, stakeholder deck, board report.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Leading with activity metrics instead of outcomes
**Symptom**: Leading with activity metrics instead of outcomes
**Problem**: Boards care about business outcomes (revenue, retention, CAC) — not effort metrics (emails sent, calls made).
**Solution**: Lead with 3-5 key outcome metrics. Explain what drove movement and what the next milestone is.

### 2. Burying risks in appendices
**Symptom**: Burying risks in appendices
**Problem**: Hiding risks or open questions at the end signals a lack of transparency and erodes board trust.
**Solution**: Surface the top 2-3 risks early with clear mitigation plans. Boards reward honesty.
