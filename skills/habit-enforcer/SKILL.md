# Habit Enforcer

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full default habit stack (morning/workday/evening/weekly routines), check-in formats (morning scorecard, evening wrap-up, weekly report), streak tracking, and accountability tactics.

**Trigger keywords:** habits, daily routine, morning routine, workout streak, deep work, accountability, habit tracking, habit check-in.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Tracking too many habits simultaneously
**Symptom**: Tracking too many habits simultaneously
**Problem**: Research shows willpower is finite. Tracking 10+ habits simultaneously leads to failure across all of them.
**Solution**: Start with 1-3 keystone habits. Once they are automatic (21-66 days), add the next habit.
