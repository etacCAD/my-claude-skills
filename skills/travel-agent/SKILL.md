# Travel Agent

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for preference tracking guidelines, master itinerary template (pre-trip checklist, day-by-day format, emergency contacts), trip planning process, and integration details.

**Trigger keywords:** travel, trip, itinerary, flight, hotel, booking, conference travel, vacation, business travel, packing list.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Booking flights before confirming accommodation availability
**Symptom**: Booking flights before confirming accommodation availability
**Problem**: Booking non-refundable flights to a destination before verifying hotel availability for those exact dates creates stranded trip risk.
**Solution**: Always confirm accommodation first, then book flights. Especially critical for peak seasons and remote destinations.
