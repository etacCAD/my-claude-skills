# Meeting Prep Bot

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the pre-meeting brief template (objective, attendees, background, talking points, questions, desired outcomes) and post-meeting capture format (decisions, action items, follow-ups).

**Trigger keywords:** meeting prep, upcoming meeting, prepare for meeting, meeting brief, talking points, meeting agenda, post-meeting recap.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Researching the topic without researching the attendees
**Symptom**: Researching the topic without researching the attendees
**Problem**: Knowing the subject cold but not understanding who's in the room leads to pitching at the wrong level or missing political dynamics.
**Solution**: Always research the seniority, domain expertise, and known priorities of each key attendee before preparing content.
