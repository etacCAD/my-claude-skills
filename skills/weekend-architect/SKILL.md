# Weekend Architect

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the weekend blueprint framework (Saturday active/social + Sunday reflective/preparatory), activity ideas by category (active, family, couple, personal recharge, social), and weekend plan template.

**Trigger keywords:** weekend plans, weekend activities, family time, date night, weekend itinerary, Saturday plans, Sunday routine.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Over-scheduling weekends
**Symptom**: Over-scheduling weekends
**Problem**: Packing every hour of a weekend creates a second workweek. The recovery value of a weekend comes from unstructured time.
**Solution**: Plan a maximum of one anchor activity per day. Leave at least 4 hours unscheduled for spontaneity and rest.
