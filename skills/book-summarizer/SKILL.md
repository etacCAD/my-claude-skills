# Book Summarizer

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the quick summary template (1-sentence takeaway, top 3 insights, frameworks, quotes), deep summary template (chapter breakdown, synthesis, action items), book categories, and recommended process.

**Trigger keywords:** book summary, summarize book, book takeaways, non-fiction summary, business book, reading list.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Summarizing chapter-by-chapter without synthesis
**Symptom**: Summarizing chapter-by-chapter without synthesis
**Problem**: A chapter-by-chapter summary is a table of contents, not a synthesis. It doesn't surface the author's core thesis.
**Solution**: Identify the 3-5 core arguments first, then map chapters to those arguments rather than summarizing linearly.

### 2. Omitting the author's counterarguments
**Symptom**: Omitting the author's counterarguments
**Problem**: Non-fiction authors often steelman opposing views before refuting them. Skipping these makes the summary feel naive.
**Solution**: Capture where the author acknowledges complexity or concedes ground — this is often where the real insight lives.
