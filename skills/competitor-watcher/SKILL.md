# Competitor Watcher

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the full monitoring framework (signal types, competitor tiers, cadence), competitive intelligence brief template, battlecard template, and integration details.

**Trigger keywords:** competitor analysis, competitive intel, battlecard, market monitoring, competitor pricing, competitor features, competitive positioning.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Monitoring press releases instead of product changes
**Symptom**: Monitoring press releases instead of product changes
**Problem**: PR is curated and delayed. Real competitive intelligence comes from product changes, job postings, and user communities.
**Solution**: Track changelog pages, G2/Capterra reviews, and competitor job descriptions to detect strategic shifts early.
