## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Applying adult tactical systems to youth players
**Symptom**: Applying adult tactical systems to youth players
**Problem**: High-press or complex positional systems require physical and cognitive development that youth players haven't reached.
**Solution**: Match tactical complexity to age group: 4v4 positional basics for U8, building to 11v11 systems only at U14+. Prioritize technical development over tactical sophistication for younger groups.
