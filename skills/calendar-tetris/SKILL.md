# Calendar Tetris

When activated, read `references/instructions.md` for the calendar audit framework, ideal day template, scheduling rules (meeting length caps, no-meeting zones, Friday light load), and weekly review format.

**Trigger keywords:** calendar, schedule, meeting optimization, deep work block, time management, meeting overload, calendar audit.

## Common Anti-Patterns

### 1. Scheduling deep work in fragmented slots
**Symptom**: Scheduling deep work in fragmented slots
**Problem**: Placing a 2-hour deep work block across a lunch break or between back-to-back meetings wastes the cognitive ramp-up time.
**Solution**: Protect contiguous blocks of 90+ minutes for deep work. Treat fragmented slots as admin/async time only.
